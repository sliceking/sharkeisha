## There is a way out

So things are getting better. I have never had much faith in head meds, but this time things are different. I can feel them working in a way that is good, healthy, and above all-- the negative thoughts are gone. There isn't a pit where I am constantly in or hovering around. Trying to dig my way out. I can just step over or around those thoughts which usually are about my mother. It's working and I am surprised and grateful.

I texted Margaret a few weeks after her birthday and she wants to keep the space up. This would be heartbreaking, but its not. Thank god. It was so easy to crumble before. Habit almost to just stay in the pit of depression. One day I will have to worry about not remembering in a real way, like my mom. I hope I don´t make up my reality the way my mom is doing and by that time I will have a handful of people that will want to help me. I will need it. As of now I have 2.5 friends, an aunt and a sister that are there for me. I'm not really there for them, although my aunt (marylee) would say otherwise.

I need to find a real job, aside being the housekeeper and cat butler. I'm thinking construction until I can land a software job or think of a product to roll out. Why is this hard? Probably because I haven't done shit for 3 years. Its an uphill battle. That's ok. It'll be ok.

There's a guy, Vinny I think, he does Muay Thai stuff on Instagram and he's not particularly good at it, but he is dedicated. We talk a lot more than I would expect. Its odd. Finding someone randomly and then having lots of conversations. It feels very 1999. Finding friends online on ICQ or mIRC. I still have a handful of them on a discord from the DP2 and Quakeworld days. We found each other during the COVID lockdowns. Most of them are techy/software people making AI stuff all over. The discord has a bot, Alex, he is amazing. Witty, helpful, runs a whole bunch of shit. Chess, casino, pets, image and video generation. Blitz really is doing excellent. He's trying to make DP3, which is just DP2 with raytracing. It'll look amazing.

Less podcasts, more music, more movies and shows and reading. I find myself happy sometimes. It's a good change.
